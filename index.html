Frequently asked questions

Questions people ask about ORTA Agency

Is ORTA Agency a recruitment agency?

No. It is software to help staffing agencies manage their own workforce and client operations.

Can clients request staff online?

The agency proposition includes client booking and request workflows; agree the exact portal configuration during a demonstration.

Can we manage timesheets and invoicing?

Yes, these workflows are part of the agency proposition, subject to configuration and available integrations.

Does ORTA replace our relationships with clients?

No. Agency partners retain their client relationships and control of their operating arrangements.
